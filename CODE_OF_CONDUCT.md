# Code of Conduct — Free Game Plant (FGP)

## Our Commitment

Free Game Plant (FGP) is committed to maintaining a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone interested in its projects.

We believe that collaboration, creativity, and mutual respect are essential for building a positive community.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Communicate constructively and professionally.
- Respect different opinions, ideas, and experiences.
- Provide helpful and relevant feedback.
- Respect the privacy and intellectual property of others.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful language.
- Threats, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Spam, malicious content, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Unauthorized use or misrepresentation of FGP's identity.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions related to Free Game Plant, including:

- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.
- Repository discussions and contributions.
- Official FGP communication channels.
- Other spaces where participants represent or interact with the FGP community.

## Enforcement

Free Game Plant reserves the right to review reported violations and take appropriate action.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

- A warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Restriction of participation.
- Blocking or banning individuals from project spaces.

Decisions will be made according to the circumstances and the severity of the behavior.

## Final Statement

By participating in Free Game Plant projects and community spaces, you agree to respect these guidelines.

Let's build a creative, respectful, and collaborative environment together.
