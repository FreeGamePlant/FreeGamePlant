# Accessibility Statement — Free Game Plant (FGP)

## Our Commitment

Free Game Plant (FGP) believes that digital experiences should be accessible, inclusive, and usable by as many people as possible.

We are committed to considering accessibility throughout the design and development of our websites, applications, games, and other digital products.

## Accessibility Principles

Our accessibility efforts are guided by the following principles:

- **Perceivable Content:** Information and interface elements should be presented in ways users can perceive.
- **Operable Interfaces:** Navigation and interactive elements should support different methods of interaction.
- **Understandable Experiences:** Content, instructions, and navigation should be clear and consistent.
- **Robust Compatibility:** Digital products should aim to work with different devices, browsers, and assistive technologies.

These principles are inspired by the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

## Development Practices

When applicable, FGP aims to:

- Use semantic HTML elements.
- Provide alternative text for meaningful images.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Provide visible focus indicators.
- Use descriptive labels for links and interactive elements.
- Avoid relying exclusively on color to communicate information.
- Respect user preferences for reduced motion.
- Design responsive interfaces for different screen sizes.

## Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process.

FGP recognizes that not every project may meet every accessibility requirement from its initial release. We aim to identify limitations, improve existing experiences, and consider accessibility in future development.

## Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you encounter an accessibility barrier in an FGP project, you can report it through:

**Free Game Plant (FGP)**  
Email: fgpfreegameplant@gmail.com

Please include a description of the issue and, if possible, the affected page or feature.

Your feedback can help us improve our digital experiences.

## Scope

This statement applies to digital products and platforms developed or maintained by Free Game Plant, including:

- Official FGP websites.
- Applications and software.
- Games and interactive experiences.
- Digital content and documentation.

Third-party platforms and services may follow their own accessibility policies.

## Final Statement

Free Game Plant is committed to learning, improving, and making accessibility an important part of its development process.

**Accessibility is part of creating technology and entertainment for everyone.**
